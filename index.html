Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Renting a Suite

What types of salon suites are available for rent at Collective 85?

Collective 85 currently has 5 private salon suites available for rent in Kirkland, WA — perfect for hairstylists, estheticians, nail techs, lash artists, massage therapists, and other beauty or wellness professionals. Each suite comes with independent plumbing (hot and cold water), individual climate control (heat and AC), soundproofed walls, and 24/7 building access. The building also houses World in a Dish (a community kitchen for cooking classes) and The Think Tank (a shared office space).

Where is Collective 85 located?

Collective 85 is located at 13027 NE 85th Street, Kirkland, WA 98033 — right on the high-traffic corridor between the Google and Microsoft campuses. The building sees over 35,000 cars daily and offers on-site parking for tenants and clients.

Does Collective 85 offer cooking classes or office space in Kirkland?

Yes! World in a Dish operates two spaces inside the Collective 85 building: a fully equipped community kitchen offering cooking classes, nutrition workshops, and private events — and The Think Tank, a shared office space for professionals who need a quiet, private workspace. Visit worldinadish.com for kitchen events and office space inquiries.

How much does it cost to rent a salon suite in Kirkland?

Salon suites at Collective 85 start at $1,650/month for interior suites and $1,750/month for window suites. All utilities are included, along with 24/7 building access, on-site parking, shared laundry, and shared restrooms. Email suite85kirkland@gmail.com or book a tour to see available suites.

Can I rent a private office near Google or Microsoft campus?

Yes! The Think Tank is a shared office space inside the Collective 85 building, operated by World in a Dish. It's located on NE 85th Street in Kirkland — the direct corridor between the Google campus in Kirkland and the Microsoft campus in Redmond. Perfect for consultants, therapists, coaches, and remote workers who want a quiet, professional workspace. Contact info@worldinadish.com for office space inquiries.
