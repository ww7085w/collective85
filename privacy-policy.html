SMS / Text Messaging

If you provide your mobile phone number and opt in (by submitting a form or our chat widget and checking the consent box), you consent to receive text messages from Collective 85 related to your inquiry — such as tour scheduling, confirmations, follow-ups, availability, and, where you have agreed, promotional updates.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in consent with any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Your mobile information is shared only with the service providers that help us deliver these messages (such as our CRM and messaging platform), strictly for that purpose.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers (e.g., our CRM, messaging, and hosting platforms) who process information on our behalf and solely to provide services to us;

As stated above, mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact us using the details below.
